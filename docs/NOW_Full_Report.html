Decision Snapshot

ServiceNow's July 22, 2026 results showed continued demand momentum: Q2 subscription revenue was USD 3.877 billion, constant-currency cRPO growth was 21.5%, and the number of customers above USD 5 million in ACV reached 658. The central public research question is whether those demand signals convert into sustained margin and cash-flow evidence in the next few quarters. ServiceNow Q2 2026 results

The Q2 evidence supports continued monitoring rather than a static conclusion. Company and Business Overview

ServiceNow sells subscription software that helps enterprises digitize and automate workflows across technology, employee, customer, creator, and security use cases. The public research focus is the company's ability to extend that workflow platform while retaining growth, large-customer expansion, and cash conversion. ServiceNow Q2 2026 results

Thesis and Variant View

The constructive view is that a broad workflow platform can deepen relationships with large enterprises, with AI and security features expanding the addressable opportunity. The counter-view is that reported demand and new-product activity may not translate into durable economic returns at the same rate.

The decision hinge is observable: future quarterly releases should sustain healthy subscription and cRPO growth while showing that operating margin and free-cash-flow conversion remain resilient. A meaningful loss of those combined signals would weaken the constructive interpretation.

Business Quality and Moat

The platform's potential differentiation rests on workflow depth, enterprise deployment, and expansion within large customers. The Q2 large-customer cohort increased from 630 to 658 customers with more than USD 5 million of ACV, which is a useful but incomplete indicator of customer expansion. Growth Drivers

  • Large-enterprise expansion: Growth in the USD 5 million-plus ACV cohort can support a continued platform-adoption narrative.
  • Subscription demand: Subscription revenue and cRPO provide the clearest recurring indicators of demand durability.
  • New-product monetization: Management reported that ServiceNow AI exceeded USD 1 billion in annual contract value during Q2; subsequent releases should show whether that activity supports durable growth and cash generation. ServiceNow Q2 2026 results

The growth case would strengthen if subscription revenue, cRPO, and the large-customer cohort continue to advance while operating and free-cash-flow margins remain resilient.
