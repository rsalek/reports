Decision Snapshot

On combines premium-brand growth, high gross margin and positive cash generation, but the evidence does not yet establish that those economics will remain stable through category, channel and geographic expansion. The research stance is HOLD / Watching, with the decision hinge defined by margin durability, cash conversion and controlled inventory.

Company and Business Overview

On is a Swiss premium athletic brand built around performance footwear, apparel and accessories sold through wholesale and direct-to-consumer channels. The economic question is whether product innovation, technical credibility and full-price demand can support a broader global platform without diluting margins or cash returns. The company's business model, supplier dependence and expansion risks are described in its 2025 Form 20-F (filed 2026-03-03).

The company is in a scale-up phase rather than a mature steady state. This creates upside from new products, owned retail and underpenetrated geographies, but also raises the burden of proof on supply chain, systems, working capital and capital allocation.

Thesis and Variant View

The constructive thesis is that On can compound through premium footwear leadership, direct customer relationships and adjacent-category growth while retaining high gross margin and improving operating leverage. The counter-view is that current brand heat and margin may be cyclically or temporarily elevated, while retail, technology and distribution expansion could raise complexity faster than returns.

The falsifiable hinge is explicit: revenue must remain resilient without an adverse inventory build; gross margin should remain above 60% and avoid a sustained breach of 58%; operating margin should remain above 10%; and operating and free cash flow should stay positive.

Business Quality and Moat

Business quality is supported by innovation-led positioning, premium pricing, full-price sell-through, direct-to-consumer growth and ample liquidity. Management attributed the latest gross-margin expansion to higher direct-to-consumer mix, operational efficiencies and full-price discipline while noting tariff pressure in the Q2 2026 filing.

Moat confidence remains medium because brand strength is difficult to measure independently and can reverse quickly in athletic products. Confidence would strengthen with broad revenue growth, stable inventory, gross margin above confirmation levels and repeatable cash conversion. It would weaken if growth required discounting or if expansion raised capital intensity without operating leverage.

Growth Drivers

  • Premium product demand: Innovation and technical credibility can sustain full-price sell-through.
  • Direct-to-consumer mix: A greater direct relationship can support margin and customer insight, provided retail capital earns an adequate return.
  • Geographic and category expansion: Underpenetrated regions, apparel and new footwear franchises broaden the addressable market.
  • Operational leverage: Scale and process efficiency can offset tariffs and continued growth investment.

The growth case strengthens only when revenue, gross margin, operating margin, inventory and cash conversion confirm together.

Monitoring Plan

At each reporting cycle, review revenue with inventory; test gross and operating margin against the stated research gates; reconcile operating cash flow, free cash flow and capital expenditure; verify liquidity; and compare management explanations for direct-to-consumer mix, full-price demand, tariffs, sourcing, foreign exchange and category expansion.

Ask three questions in order: Is demand healthy without inventory stress? Are premium economics surviving scale? Is reported profit converting into cash? The stance strengthens only if all three answers remain supported by dated primary evidence.

Decision Hinge

The decision hinges on whether premium brand economics persist through scale: gross margin must remain above warning levels, operating margin must hold above 10%, and operating and free cash flow must stay positive while inventory and capital intensity remain controlled.

Company Thesis

On can compound as a premium global athletic brand if innovation, full-price sell-through and direct-to-consumer growth continue to support high gross margin while category and geographic expansion remain cash generative.

Security Thesis and Intrinsic Framing

ONON is a watchlist security rather than an actionable position. Intrinsic outcomes span a wide range and the locked analysis retains a soft risk block, so strong company quality is not yet sufficient for a stronger recommendation.

Variant perception: On may possess better brand economics and operating leverage than a conventional footwear company, but evidence does not yet prove that those economics remain stable through broader category, channel and geographic scale.

Falsifiable Thesis Pillars

1. Premium brand supports durable growth

Premium product innovation and full-price demand can sustain broad revenue growth without an adverse inventory build.

  • Tracked KPIs: Revenue, Inventory, Gross margin
  • Decision thresholds: Gross margin warning threshold — < 0.6 ratio
  • Falsifier: Revenue decelerates while inventory rises and gross margin falls below the 60% warning threshold.
  • Confidence: medium
  • Evidence: On Holding Q2 2026 Form 6-K (as of 2026-08-11); On Holding 2025 Form 20-F (as of 2026-03-03)

2. Scale converts into margin expansion

Direct-to-consumer mix, full-price discipline and operating leverage can sustain high gross margin and expand operating profitability despite tariffs and growth investment.

  • Tracked KPIs: Gross margin, Operating margin, Operating income / EBIT
  • Decision thresholds: Gross margin confirm threshold — >= 0.62 ratio, Gross margin break threshold — < 0.58 ratio, Operating margin warning threshold — < 0.1 ratio
  • Falsifier: Gross margin remains below 58% or operating margin remains below 10% without a credible temporary explanation.
  • Confidence: medium
  • Evidence: On Holding Q2 2026 Form 6-K (as of 2026-08-11); On Holding 2025 Form 20-F (as of 2026-03-03)

3. Cash conversion supports self-funded expansion

Positive operating and free cash flow plus net cash liquidity can make expansion self-funded and resilient.

  • Tracked KPIs: Operating cash flow, Free cash flow, Cash and equivalents, Total debt
  • Decision thresholds: Operating cash flow break threshold — < CHF 0, Free cash flow warning threshold — < CHF 0
  • Falsifier: Operating cash flow turns negative, or free cash flow weakens persistently alongside rising inventory and capital expenditure.
  • Confidence: medium
  • Evidence: On Holding Q2 2026 Form 6-K (as of 2026-08-11)

Catalysts

  • Profitable growth remains above confirmation thresholds: Broad full-price revenue growth with gross margin at or above 62% and operating margin at or above 12% would strengthen evidence that brand demand is converting into durable economics. — On Holding Q2 2026 Form 6-K (as of 2026-08-11)

  • Cash conversion funds expansion: Positive operating and free cash flow through continued retail, product and supply-chain investment would demonstrate self-funded expansion. — On Holding Q2 2026 Form 6-K (as of 2026-08-11)

Risks and Counter-Evidence

  • Demand and category concentration: Discretionary demand, premium pricing and footwear concentration can create simultaneous revenue, inventory and markdown pressure. — On Holding 2025 Form 20-F (as of 2026-03-03)

  • Supply chain, tariffs and currency: Third-party manufacturing and logistics, tariffs and foreign exchange can compress margin and cash generation even when demand remains healthy. — On Holding 2025 Form 20-F (as of 2026-03-03); On Holding Q2 2026 Form 6-K (as of 2026-08-11)

  • Expansion execution and governance: Rapid retail, category, technology and geographic expansion may raise capital intensity or dilute returns; governance remains an underwriting constraint. — On Holding 2025 Form 20-F (as of 2026-03-03)
