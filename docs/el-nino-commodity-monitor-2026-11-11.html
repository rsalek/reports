Forecast status

Strong to very strong El Niño; peak and decay path still unchanged

FactBOM, issued 10 November, measured weekly relative Niño3.4 at +2.75°C for the week ending 8 November, slightly below the event's +2.88°C weekly peak. Its 30-day SOI was −14.4 and the weekly IOD index +0.75°C. All surveyed models still put the El Niño peak in November or December and keep values above thresholds into austral autumn 2027. BMKG, 3 November, remains the latest Indonesian dekadal analysis at Niño3.4 +3.57°C and ASO +3.09°C. IRI, 19 October, retains 100% El Niño probability from OND through MAM 2027. NOAA, 8 October, retains an 83% chance that OND RONI reaches at least +2.5°C; its next discussion is due 12 November.

InferenceThe weekly ocean reading cooled slightly, but the central forecast did not. A single weekly move from +2.88°C to +2.75°C is not evidence of an early decay. The expected sequence remains a November–December peak followed by gradual weakening during boreal spring 2027. Forecast confidence remains very high; crop-loss confidence must still come from crop-region observations and balances.

Regions and crop calendars

Where the forecast meets sensitive windows

Indonesia and Malaysia | palm oil

Harvest is year-round and weather transmits to yields with a lag. South Kalimantan improved from predominantly low rain in late October to 58% medium rain in early November, while South Sumatra remained mostly below normal. The record 3.621 Mt Malaysian stock cushion pushes the scarcity test into a multi-month output-and-drawdown sequence.

Vietnam and Indonesia | robusta

Vietnam's main harvest is under way. The 11 November outlook turns the focus from short-term rain disruption to below-normal Central Highlands rainfall through 10 December. That is a future moisture watch; record 2025/26 robusta exports and 1.13m bags of London stocks are the current balance.

Brazil | arabica and sugar

Flowering and fruit set remain sensitive to November moisture. The new ICO evidence shows arabica's immediate constraint is exceptionally low certified stocks, while recent rain supported flowering. This is distinct from the better-supplied robusta channel and from São Paulo cane conditions.

India and Thailand | sugar

India's October-start and Thailand's December-start crushing seasons make cane weight, sucrose recovery, reservoir levels and mill throughput the next proof points. The tighter U.S. balance is not evidence of Asian El Niño loss.

Southern Africa | maize

October to early January is the main planting window. Intended 2027 white-maize area is down 18.18%, and USDA now projects only 15.0 Mt of South African corn in 2026/27. The record 17.691 Mt current commercial crop and higher opening stocks remain near-term buffers.

Ghana and Côte d'Ivoire | cocoa

The October–March main crop is under way. No new public ICCO current-season balance was available. Arrivals, pod disease, bean quality, grindings and the November bulletin—not price commentary—must establish a change.

Global corn, wheat and rice

U.S. corn harvest reached 81% through 8 November versus 86% average, while winter-wheat condition improved to 49% good/excellent. November WASDE raised global corn, wheat, rice and total-grain stocks, offsetting the U.S. corn production cut.

ETF/ETC recommendation implications

Wait for physical tightening before adding exposure

Editorial addition dated 13 November 2026, based on the original 11 November, 14:38 GMT evidence cutoff. This is not a fresh market update. The products discussed here are commodity ETCs, rather than equity ETFs. The recommendation is to keep them on watch and require a matching physical thesis before considering new exposure.

AGAP / AIGA agriculture basket

Wait. Case weakened; medium confidence. Higher world grain stocks and record Malaysian palm inventories reduce the case for broad scarcity exposure. A basket also dilutes regional robusta and palm risks. Reconsider if several official commodity balances tighten together and inventories fall across successive releases.

AIGS softs basket

Watch. Case unchanged; medium confidence. Mixed sugar and cocoa evidence does not yet establish basket-wide tightening. Its Coffee C component follows arabica, so Indonesian or Vietnamese robusta stress alone is insufficient. Reconsider after corroborated sugar, cocoa or arabica production and export deterioration.

COFF coffee

Avoid as a robusta proxy. Watch for an independent arabica case. Low U.S. arabica certified stocks merit attention, while stronger robusta exports weaken the original regional scarcity argument. Confidence is high on the exposure mismatch and medium on the investment implication. Require Brazilian crop evidence, arabica exports and persistent certified-stock tightening before considering exposure.

SUGA sugar

Watch. No upgrade; medium confidence. The tighter U.S. sugar balance does not establish a deficit in the global raw-sugar futures market tracked by this ETC. Require India and Thailand crush or recovery evidence, Brazilian export supply and a corroborated global balance deterioration.

COCO cocoa

Wait. Evidence insufficient; low confidence. A newer public ICCO balance and West African arrival data are needed before upgrading the case. Reconsider if arrivals, quality and grindings establish a sustained deficit. Improving arrivals or weaker grindings would weaken the case.

CORN / AIGG grains

Wait. Global shortage case weakened; medium confidence. USDA raised world corn and total-grain stocks despite U.S. and South African output cuts. Neither route captures South African white-maize basis directly. Reconsider after repeated global stock reductions or confirmed exporter shortfalls; retain regional maize monitoring separately.

Implementation conditions. No direct palm-oil or robusta product was verified in the bounded catalogue search. Do not substitute an unrelated basket. Before any purchase, recheck the exact ISIN, current access, spread, futures curve and issuer documents. These ETCs track futures returns, so roll yield, fees, currency movements and counterparty structure can change returns materially. A GBP trading line does not by itself remove underlying currency exposure. Single-commodity ETCs concentrate risk; baskets can dilute the physical signal. Decision gates

Strengthen, weaken, invalidate

Strengthen

Indonesian palm-zone deficits persist beyond November and MPOB output falls while the 3.621 Mt stock cushion draws for several months; Vietnam's below-normal Central Highlands rain is followed by weaker arrivals, exports or crop estimates; India or Thailand cane recovery is cut; cocoa arrivals disappoint; realised South African white-maize area approaches the −18.18% intention with delayed establishment; or several official balances tighten together.

Weaken

South Kalimantan's rain recovery broadens; MPOB stocks remain above 3 Mt; London robusta stocks stay near or above 1m bags and Vietnam exports remain strong; South African producers plant above intentions with adequate rain; Asian sugar recovery holds; world grain stocks stay near the November forecasts; or cocoa arrivals improve.

Invalidate

A very strong El Niño passes without adverse target-region crop anomalies; official balances keep improving into early 2027; observed price moves are better explained by policy, logistics, FX, energy or positioning; or no UK-accessible instrument matches the confirmed physical channel.
