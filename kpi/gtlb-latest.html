Catalysts

  • Enterprise expansionContinued high-teens growth in customers above $100,000 ARR would improve growth durability and operating leverage.
  • GAAP margin inflectionSustained revenue growth with tighter operating expense growth could convert the high gross margin into positive GAAP operating income.

Risks

  • Retention moderationDollar-based net retention of 117% remains healthy but is below earlier levels, increasing dependence on new-customer and seat growth.

  • Equity dilutionShare-based compensation and changes in diluted share count can offset gains in enterprise value on a per-share basis.

Monitoring triggers

  • Cash and profitability conversionMonitor free cash flow, GAAP operating margin, liquidity and diluted shares together for evidence of durable per-share economics.
