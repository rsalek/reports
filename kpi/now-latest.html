Thesis signals

Thesis pillars

  • Demand and backlogSubscription revenue rose from Q1 and constant-currency cRPO accelerated modestly to 21.5% in Q2.
  • Enterprise expansionCustomers above USD 5 million ACV increased from 630 at March 31 to 658 at June 30.
  • Cash-conversion disciplineQ2 free-cash-flow margin was lower than Q1; future margin recovery is the key confirmation test alongside growth.

Catalysts

  • AI monetization scaleServiceNow AI crossed USD 1 billion in annual contract value in Q2; later results need to show conversion into durable revenue and cash flow.
  • Large-customer expansionFurther net additions to the USD 5 million-plus ACV cohort would support cross-sell and platform adoption.

Risks

  • Cash-margin compressionThe Q2 free-cash-flow margin was 16%, below the Q1 seasonal level, so growth must be assessed with cash conversion rather than revenue alone.
  • Acquisition and delivery costsManagement disclosed acquisition-related margin headwinds; integration and AI delivery costs remain monitoring risks.

Monitoring triggers

  • Demand durabilityReview subscription revenue and constant-currency cRPO together after each quarterly release.
  • Margin and cash conversionReview non-GAAP operating margin and free-cash-flow margin together; do not infer durable cash conversion from a single quarter.
