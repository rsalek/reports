Quality-of-print bridge

Headline growth
15% reported revenue growth versus 12% organic; organic revenue excludes FX and trailing-12-month non-CGM acquisitions or divestitures.
Geographic mix
U.S. grew 11%, while international grew 26% reported and 17% organic.
Margin quality
Gross margin improved on manufacturing efficiency, production volume, freight normalization, mix, and early G7 15-Day benefit.
Input-cost caveat
Management described 50-100 bps of potential fuel and resin risk within the year.
Cash-flow caveat
$525.6m operating cash flow was strong, but working-capital timing contributed to the year-over-year change.
Disclosure gap
No precise Q1 net-addition, active-user, retention, utilization, or Stelo revenue series was provided.

What proves or breaks the setup

Proves it

Q2 stays within the full-year growth path, U.S. growth stabilizes or improves, G7 conversion progresses, international remains above company growth, and non-GAAP gross margin stays within or above 63%-64%.

Breaks it

U.S. growth weakens despite coverage, conversion or reliability disappoints, Stelo remains opaque, and gross margin falls below guidance at the same time.

Still missing

An internal forecast and FCF model, peer cross-check, explicit CapEx/SBC/dilution treatment, Investor Day slides, full CONNECT publication, and quantitative active-base, reliability, retention, utilization, and Stelo data.
