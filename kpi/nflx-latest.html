Thesis pillars

  • Global scale compounds revenuePricing, engagement, advertising and global distribution sustain revenue above the quarterly warning gate while funding the content slate.

  • Cash conversion catches up with earningsFree cash flow recovers from quarterly volatility and supports reinvestment and capital returns without dependence on rising leverage.

Catalysts

  • Advertising and pricing executionFaster monetisation with stable engagement can sustain revenue growth and operating leverage.
  • Cash conversion recoverySequential FCF recovery would reduce the main gap between earnings quality and valuation support.

Risks
