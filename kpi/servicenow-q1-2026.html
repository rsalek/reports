EPS quality screen

No material EPS-quality trigger was identified for the KPI decision in this packet. The analysis therefore anchors on subscription revenue, cRPO, customer/deal indicators, operating margin, and free cash flow rather than headline EPS. GAAP-to-non-GAAP reconciliation remains significant because stock compensation, acquired-intangible amortization, business-combination costs, and severance materially affect operating income. ER pp.13-17

What proves or breaks the setup

Proves it

Q2 cRPO at or above 19.5% CC, organic cRPO at or above Goldman's roughly 17.25% bar, subscription growth at or above 21%, continued >100% Now Assist cohort growth, and stable large-deal activity.

Breaks it

Both cRPO and subscription growth miss guide, renewal falls below 97%, and large deals weaken together. That combination would point to demand, not timing noise.

Still missing

An audited internal FCFF bridge, detailed estimate revisions and cRPO consensus, the complete Goldman note, and repeat disclosure supporting product-vector ACV.
