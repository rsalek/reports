Thesis signals

Thesis pillars

  • AI and EUV demandAI-related Logic and Memory investment is converting into stronger system demand and a raised 2026 revenue outlook.
  • Installed-base monetizationSequential growth in service and field-option sales reinforces the recurring installed-base flywheel.
  • Margin durability and cash conversionMargin is strong and guided higher, but H1 working-capital absorption must reverse for earnings quality to remain supportive.

Catalysts

  • Q3 guide executionDelivery of Q3 sales at or above the €11 billion floor with gross margin at or above 55% would confirm the accelerated 2026 trajectory.
  • Capacity conversionPlanned low-NA EUV and DUV immersion capacity additions can convert strong customer commitments into 2027-2028 deliveries.

Risks

  • Export controls and geopoliticsExport restrictions and geopolitical policy can limit product and service access even when end demand remains strong.
  • Working-capital absorptionH1 operating cash flow was negative despite strong profit, making inventory and working-capital conversion a near-term quality test.
  • Capacity and product-ramp executionCapacity expansion and High-NA/low-NA system ramps must meet customer throughput, timing and quality requirements.

Monitoring triggers

  • Demand conversionTrack whether system delivery and quarterly sales remain consistent with the raised full-year range.
  • Margin durabilityEscalate if gross margin falls below the full-year guide floor without a clearly temporary mix or ramp explanation.
  • Cash conversion and liquidityRequire H2 working-capital conversion while preserving net-cash balance-sheet strength.
  • Installed-base monetizationTrack sequential growth in service and field-option sales as the installed base expands.
