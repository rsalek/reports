How the library fits together

Move from a company thesis to operating evidence and then to cross-company context without leaving the research index.

What is here

  • An analysis workflow guide for preparing sources, running or resuming Phase 0, and validating citations.
  • Company reports covering thesis, business quality, moat, growth, valuation, risks, and action framework.
  • KPI monitors covering baseline metrics, thresholds, catalysts, scenarios, and next-proof checks.
  • A connected wiki for recurring cross-company themes, plus an archive for earlier standalone pages.

A practical reading order

  • Start with the workflow guide for a new ticker or a local run in progress.
  • Open a company report to understand the current thesis, rating, phase, and action framework.
  • Open a KPI monitor to track operating proof, thresholds, catalysts, and thesis changes.
  • Use the wiki to compare recurring themes, then return here to move between stocks.
