Collection overview

This collection combines full investment reports, KPI monitoring dashboards, and a connected knowledge wiki into one research layer for reviewing theses, valuation assumptions, risks, catalysts, and follow-up questions.

What the collection contains

  • Full company reports covering executive summaries, business phase classification, moat analysis, growth drivers, valuation, risk, antifragility, and buy-plan modules.
  • KPI dashboards that turn new evidence into explicit thresholds, scenario ranges, thesis changes, and next-proof requirements.
  • Hyperlinked cards for each stock, including local repository reports and externally hosted reports where the source file is still in Drive.
  • An interactive wiki application for connecting recurring themes across reports, including AI disruption, platform economics, capital returns, valuation sensitivity, and behavioural decision checks.
  • Archive access for earlier standalone pages so previous analyses remain reachable without cluttering the main research index.

How to use the site

  • Start with a report card to open the full company analysis and review the current thesis, rating, phase, and action framework.
  • Use a KPI dashboard for the current operating baseline, expectation bar, catalysts, and evidence-change log.
  • Use the wiki to move across companies by concept, such as moat quality, FCF yield, AI exposure, M&A risk, or portfolio sizing discipline.
  • Return to the index when comparing reports side by side or checking which analyses are local HTML files versus linked external sources.
